# modelcards.net — card art prompts

One image per card, generated with **Gemini 2.5 Flash Image ("Nano Banana")**, aspect ratio **4:3**. Crop the bottom-right `✦` watermark before use; the warm-on-dark background drops straight into each card's art window.

**The system:** one art style for the whole set (painterly key-art, chiaroscuro, emerging-from-shadow) + a shared gold-spark/glyph signature on every card. Each lab is its own world with its own palette; the creature's form encodes the model's role (reasoner / agent / multimodal / swarm / keeper). Tier sets grandeur (apex → primordial).

**The six worlds:** Anthropic = the Scholar Order · OpenAI = the Awakening Foundry · Google DeepMind = the Astral Observatory · Meta AI = the Celestial Herd · Mistral AI = the Storm Court · xAI = the Void-Born.

**Status:** `Claude Opus 4.8` already has the wizard; `Claude Opus 4.7` (dragon) and `Claude Sonnet 4.6` (sphinx) are bonus cards already generated. Everything else is `todo`.

### Claude 2  ·  `claude-2`  ·  todo

```text
Premium fantasy trading-card character illustration for a holo-rare collector card: an ancient, weathered, primordial robed archmage of the scholar-order. Painterly semi-realism with the finish of gilded gold-foil card key art crossed with an old illuminated manuscript: confident brushwork, fine gold-ink filigree, a faint etched/engraved quality to the linework.

Subject: an ancient, weathered, primordial robed archmage of the scholar-order, turned three-quarters toward the viewer, emerging from deep shadow, monumental and still; marked as a reasoner, threads of glowing thought weave around its head and it holds an oracle's deep, knowing gaze. behind it the faint shelves and arches of an arcane library dissolve into the dark.

Arcane detail: fine luminous golden filaments and faint glowing glyphs drift around it; a small radiant many-rayed gold spark sigil hovers near it (an arcane emblem of intelligence, not any real-world brand mark) casting light across it; a few translucent manuscript pages drift at the edges and dissolve into the dark.

Palette: warm copper, bronze and amber on a deep espresso-to-near-black brown ground warming to burnt amber toward the centre; the gold spark sits naturally in the warmth.

Lighting: dramatic chiaroscuro, one warm light from the upper-left plus the spark's golden glow, strong rim-light separating it from the dark, corners falling into a near-black vignette so it reads as emerging from shadow.

Composition: landscape 4:3, the subject centred with generous margin so it is never cropped, the spark near the upper-centre, dark negative space in the corners. Monumental, iconic, still.

Avoid: any text, letters or numbers, real brand logos, card frames or borders, watermark or signature, modern objects, no cool colours (no blue, teal, green or purple dominating), pure white, cluttered backgrounds, cartoon / chibi / anime styling, cropped or distorted anatomy.
```
