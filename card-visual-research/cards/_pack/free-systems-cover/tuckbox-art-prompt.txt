Premium fantasy trading-card pack cover key art: the creatures of one card set gathered into a single overlapping crest. Painterly semi-realism with the finish of gilded gold-foil card key art crossed with an old illuminated manuscript: confident brushwork, fine gold-ink filigree, a faint etched quality to the linework.

Arcane detail: a small radiant many-rayed gold spark sigil burns where the bodies cross, casting warm light across all of them; fine luminous golden filaments and faint glyphs thread between the creatures; a few translucent manuscript pages drift at the edges and dissolve into the dark.

Palette: deep near-black background, warm gold and bronze dominant, each creature carrying a faint tint of its own lab colour (emerald, violet, indigo, orange, pale blue).
