ABOUT THIS SET

Every model in this set ships with a document its own lab wrote about it. Those documents are the industry's record of what a model can do and what it was tested for. Almost nobody reads them.

They are also wildly uneven. The longest card here runs 66,211 words. The shortest runs 1,340. Some report dozens of benchmarks and some report none at all.

So we read them. One card per model, every figure taken from the lab's own published document. Where a lab reported nothing, the card says nothing.

The set runs in release order, so you can read along the shelf and watch capability climb while the disclosure behind it does whatever it happens to do. The two do not move together.

Laid out side by side they show the shape of the record: the handful of benchmarks every lab reports, and the much larger number only one lab has ever run. What a lab chose not to measure is printed here too.
